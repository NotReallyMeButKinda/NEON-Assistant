# NEON-Assistant
Large, completely-local vibecoded replacement for google assistant with an absolute metric shit ton of features
Written in a week with claude code, NOT HUMAN WRITTEN.
# Why, features, and how it works.
Google, Alexa, Microsoft, and other companies have made the shittiest AIs to ever exist.
* copilot is shit
* google killed assistant in favor of gemini, which is way slower and more shit.
* cortana was also shit
* alexa is doing the same shit that google does, but they harvest more data
* most companies put a shitty chatbot (sometimes) with voice capabilities into their app.

the biggest pain point of these ais is they dont attempt to handle anything locally, they dont attempt to grab information quick before spending time querying an ai. NEON attempts to ask needle what a prompt is trying to do, depending on what it's trying to do it attempts to pass to one of a handful of tools:
* Calculator
* Built-in Trello clone
* Memory/reminders
* Web Search w/ Ollama and Wikipedia, or a search api.
* Timers
* Fun
* Weather lookup
* Time lookup
* App opener
* Everything-supported search
* Bitwarden
* Elaboration
* Misc. (settings and stuff, yknow?)
## Notifications
It can also take notifications from windows and format them or summarize them for tts, add them to the Trello clone, not summarize them and just read them out loud, just show them in the built-in status bar, or just be muted.
Status bar? OH YEAH, the status bar.
## Status bar
Includes a status bar that can be configured to show a clock, weather, the AI's output, a waveform when it speaks, the status of the AI, buttons to control the AI, notifications, and your input. Works similarly to a plasma panel. and is an alternative to the shade.

## Shade
Status bar too heavy? use the Shade, shows when the AI has something to say, with it's output and a spotlight-like background, and can also be configured to show when the status bar can not be shown (like in fullscreen).

## Trello Clone
I don't like Trello, its okay software, but it lacks a good way to easily add cards with due dates, and requires loading a full webpage. Add a Card in the board by typing what the card should say and the date, you can also ask the AI to do this for you.

## Other features
* Wake word support, by either constantly running whisper or using OpenWakeWord.
* Custom regex for filtering notification output before it hits the summary AI.
* Support for 3 "layers" of ai, starting at needle, then onto an Ollama model, and then another Ollama model.
* Configure literally fucking everything, from colors to sounds, extremely precise and how you want.
* Multiple personalities to pick from.
* A browser extension that allows the AI to read web pages.
* Welcome setup screen.
* Smooth animations for most things.
* Completely native QT6-based ui.
* Works offline.
* Does basically everything google assistant can do, and more.
* HomeAssistant Support (completely untested, I don't have home assistant).
* Replacement for the copilot key.
